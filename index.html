Structured Physical Intelligence

Foundation models that perceive structure and learn through interaction.

Seung Hyun Lee is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Stella X. Yu. His research focuses on developing foundation models for visual and embodied intelligence, bridging human perception with machine understanding and physical interaction.

His work spans structure-aware visual representation, generative modeling, and robot learning. He has interned at Google DeepMind and Google Research. At Google Research, he developed methods for improving image generation and cropping using perceptual quality signals.
