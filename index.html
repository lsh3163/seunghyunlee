Seung Hyun Lee · Ph.D. Candidate

Learning structure for visual and embodied intelligence.

I develop models that connect structured perception, generative modeling, and physical interaction.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Stella X. Yu. I have interned at Google DeepMind and Google Research; at Google Research, I developed methods for improving image generation and cropping using perceptual quality signals.

PRISM: Polynomial Representations for Interaction-Structured Motor Control

PRISM makes polynomial interactions among observable physical variables explicit and learnable, improving locomotion and contact-rich manipulation without adding sensors.

SHED Light on Segmentation for Dense Prediction
